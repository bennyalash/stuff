Game Overview

Each player races to feed Mama more than anyone else. On your turn you collect resources, put animal and plant cards into play, and use them to build a stronger engine. Every time you pay Food to feed Mama, you move closer to victory, but the next meal costs more Food for everyone.

The first player to feed Mama the required number of times wins.

Setup

1

Prepare the deck

Remove the Point Tracking Card and the 3 Quick Rule cards. Shuffle the rest and deal 3 cards to each player. Keep your hand hidden.

2

Set up the points

Place the Point Tracking Card face up in the middle of the play area. Put every player’s marker on its start space.

3

Set up the Food Board

Place the Food Board in the middle of the play area and put one Food (brown cylinder) on its start space. This is the Food Tracker.

4

Make the bank

Place all remaining Food and Coins in the middle. This shared pile is the bank. Keep the draw pile nearby with room for a discard pile.

5

Pick the first player

Everyone rolls the die. The highest roller goes first, then play continues clockwise.

6

Give out bonus resources

Later players get a head start to balance the game.

Winning

Reach the winning space on the Point Tracking Card and you have Fully Fed ’Yer Mama. You win the game immediately. The winning space depends on the number of players.

Your Turn

Follow these four steps in order every turn. Each step is optional.

1

Gain

Choose one:

  • Gain 2 Coins from the middle, or
  • Draw 1 Card randomly from the deck, or
  • Gain 1 Food from the middle.
2

Use your cards’ abilities

For each card you have bought and placed in front of you, you may use the ability written on it. Use them in any order. You don’t have to use them all.

The portion in italics is not part of the ability. It is a note that is always true of the card. For example, if the Wolf tries to kill the Cat, the italics on the Cat say it cannot be killed.

3

Play card(s)

Pay using the cost in the card’s top corners: top left is the Coin cost, top right is the Food cost. Put the spent Coins and Food back in the middle, then place the card in front of you.

You can use the card’s ability on later turns. The italic portion takes effect immediately.

4

Feed ’Yer Mama

  • Pay the Food shown on the next space of the Food Board after the Food Tracker’s current space.
  • Move your marker up one space on the Point Tracking Card.
  • Move the Food Tracker up one space, so the next meal costs more.

You may do this once per turn.

Rules & Exceptions

  • No Coins left? You may steal Coins from other players. Example: you need to gain 4 Coins but only 2 are in the middle. Take the 2, then take 1 Coin from 2 players or 2 Coins from 1 player.
  • No Food left? Steal Food from other players the same way. See the Pig for an exception.
  • No cards left to draw? Do nothing.
  • Deck runs out? Reshuffle all cards that have been discarded for any reason.
  • Italic abilities are not part of a turn’s action. They are always true of the card.

Card Clarifications

PLANT

Corn

You cannot copy another player’s animal.

Copy only the part of the ability that is not italicized.

You can either gain 1 Coin or pay 2 Coins to copy, not both.

ANIMAL

Dog

The Wolf can kill the Dog. Once the Dog is dead, your other animals can also be killed.

You can kill your own Pig even if you have a Dog.

With 2 Dogs, none of your animals can be killed, including the Dogs.

ANIMAL

Goat

You decide which plant to destroy after rolling the die.

The number on the die must match the Coin cost of the plant. For example, a 5 can only destroy a Tomato.

ANIMAL

Fox

You cannot copy one of your own animals. Copy only the non-italic part. If you copy the Dog, ignore its italics about your other animals.

You can copy another Fox, but then you must do exactly what that ability says and pay another Coin for this Fox. This lets you copy one of your own animals, which a Fox normally cannot.

ANIMAL

Pig

Gain 1 Food and place it on the Pig card. Food there cannot be spent. It cannot be stolen by the Raccoon or by other players when the Food runs out.*

You can kill your own Pig even if you have a Dog. When the Pig is killed, count the Food on it, multiply by 3, and gain that much Food.

*It can be stolen if that is the only way a player can gain Food, meaning no one else has Food.

ANIMAL

Wolf

You decide which animal to kill after rolling the die.

Ignore the Food cost. Compare your roll only with the Coin cost. If the Coin cost is lower than your roll, kill the animal.

You cannot kill the Cat.

ANIMAL

Chicken

Count the Chickens you have in play, including this one. Gain that much Food.

If you have multiple Chickens, each one counts toward the total.

Advanced Rules

Recommended only after playing multiple times. Use them one at a time or together.

Rule Change #1: The Market

Deal only 1 card to each player at the start. Place 3 cards face up in the middle. Players may pay for these as if they were in hand, but they cannot be drawn. When one is bought, replace it.

Rule Change #2: Roll to Shop

At the start of each turn, instead of step 1, roll the die and gain temporary buying power equal to your roll. Spend it immediately:

CostResources
11 Coin
21 Card
31 Food
42 Coins
52 Cards
62 Food

You cannot buy from the same row twice.

Rule Change #3: Lucky and Unlucky Rolls

  • If you ever roll a 1 for any reason, steal 1 Coin.
  • If you ever roll a 6 for any reason, give another player 1 of your Coins.
